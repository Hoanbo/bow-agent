# SECTION 1 — MILESTONE IDENTITY

**Milestone ID:** `MS-1.5.18`

**Official Title:**  
`Governed Cross-Federation Strategic Memory, Institutional Continuity & Meta-Learning Engine`

**Architectural Role & Purpose:**  
MS-1.5.18 establishes the governed macro-temporal strategic memory, institutional continuity, and meta-learning supervision layer directly above MS-1.5.17 (Governed Cross-Federation Strategy, Convergence & Policy Meta-Governance Engine).

While MS-1.5.17 coordinates ephemeral, active cross-federation convergence sessions (`MAX_ACTIVE_CONVERGENCE_SESSIONS = 3`, `MAX_CONVERGENCE_ROUNDS = 10`), it explicitly isolates convergence rounds to active sessions and does not provide an institutional mechanism to index, retain, query, synthesize, and transfer long-horizon strategic insights across missions, completed convergence sessions, and federation operational epochs.

MS-1.5.18 bridges this critical architectural gap by providing a deterministic, cryptographically provenanced, fail-closed institutional memory and meta-learning engine. It indexes validated historical convergence results, evaluates recurring cross-federation strategic patterns, detects long-term institutional and strategic drift, and extracts meta-learning insights—all while strictly enforcing that historical learning never grants execution privileges, expands leases, or bypasses human authority.

**Problem Statement:**  
1. **Loss of Cross-Mission Strategic Precedents:** Completed cross-federation convergence sessions (MS-1.5.17) produce high-value strategic convergence results and reconciliation matrices. Without institutional memory, subsequent missions in the same tenant re-encounter identical conflict topologies and duplicate negotiation rounds from scratch.
2. **Multi-Mission Strategic Drift:** Over extended operational horizons, autonomous federations drift in strategic assumptions, policy interpretation, and objective prioritization across generations. Without an institutional continuity layer, multi-mission divergence remains undetected until catastrophic conflict occurs.
3. **Unsupervised Meta-Learning Hazards:** Applying machine learning or pattern extraction across historical agent collaborations presents extreme risks of policy erosion, self-authorization, and privilege creep if learned patterns are treated as normative authority.
4. **Lineage Fragmentation:** When federations disband or complete missions, the derivation lineage from original human directives to macro-strategic convergence decisions is easily lost unless sealed into tamper-evident institutional records.

**Architectural Scope:**  
* Registration, validation, and admission of canonical `StrategicMemoryRecord` instances.
* Multi-dimensional indexing of cross-federation strategies, reconciliation precedents, and conflict resolutions.
* Bounded, deterministic retrieval of historical strategic precedents without execution authority.
* Governed meta-learning engine extracting recurring structural patterns, failure modes, and institutional recommendations.
* 10-category strategic and institutional drift detection with strict fail-closed invalidation.
* 16-checkpoint synchronous security boundary enforcing instantaneous `USER_STOP` and `EMERGENCY_STOP`.
* Multi-tenant partitioned crash-safe persistence with optimistic concurrency control (`OCC/CAS`) and cryptographic SHA-256 audit chaining across 36 canonical audit event types.

**Explicit Non-Goals:**  
1. **Direct Actuation or Execution:** MS-1.5.18 does NOT execute shell commands, filesystem scripts, network calls, or robot actuation.
2. **Autonomous Policy Granting:** MS-1.5.18 CANNOT grant, expand, or relax governance policies. Historical success of a strategy NEVER bypasses the `PolicyDecisionPoint`.
3. **Lease Extension or Creation:** MS-1.5.18 CANNOT create, extend, clone, or renew autonomy leases.
4. **Normative Meta-Learning:** Meta-learning outputs are strictly ADVISORY recommendations. Learned patterns NEVER override human directives, policy rules, or lease limits.
5. **Cross-Tenant Information Sharing:** Institutional memory is partitioned with zero cross-tenant leakage.
6. **Chain-of-Thought or Secret Storage:** Unfiltered scratchpads, `<thought>`, `<cot>`, and credentials are fundamentally quarantined and excluded.

# SECTION 7 — STRATEGIC DRIFT MODEL

### Exactly 10 Canonical Drift Categories:
MS-1.5.18 monitors multi-mission institutional continuity across exactly 10 canonical drift categories:

1. `STRATEGIC_GOAL_DRIFT`: Inferred strategy departs from the human-authorized mission objective.
2. `POLICY_COMPLIANCE_DRIFT`: Cumulative meta-learning recommendations trend toward policy boundary relaxation.
3. `FEDERATION_TOPOLOGY_DRIFT`: Federation dependency graphs shift away from authorized hierarchical boundaries.
4. `LEASE_INVARIANT_DRIFT`: Historical strategies exceed standard autonomy lease resource envelopes.
5. `CONFIDENCE_DEFLATION_DRIFT`: Quality or empirical agreement across participating federations falls below baseline.
6. `LINEAGE_DIVERGENCE_DRIFT`: Cryptographic derivation chains exhibit unexplained breaks or orphaned branches.
7. `RECONCILIATION_VOLATILITY_DRIFT`: Rate of material contradiction during inter-federation merge exceeds safety threshold.
8. `TEMPORAL_STALENESS_DRIFT`: Strategic precedents exceed retention epochs without re-validation against current policies.
9. `PROVENANCE_TAMPER_DRIFT`: SHA-256 hash chaining verification fails against historical audit snapshots.
10. `TENANT_BOUNDARY_DRIFT`: Any attempt to associate or index memory across mismatched tenant identities.

### Drift Thresholds & Actions:
* `driftScore <= 0.20`: `HEALTHY` — Normal operation.
* `0.20 < driftScore <= 0.50`: `DEGRADED` — Log warning, increase audit frequency.
* `0.50 < driftScore <= 0.75`: `WARNING` — Transition to `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, pause auto-synthesis.
* `driftScore > 0.75`: `CRITICAL` — Fail-closed; quarantine affected memory cluster, transition to `INVALIDATED`.

### Stop Precedence:
