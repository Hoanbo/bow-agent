# SECTION 4 — PRIMARY ARCHITECTURAL GAPS & SOLUTIONS

### GAP A: Advisory Mediation & Admission
* **Gap:** MS-1.5.18 emits advisory recommendations (`GovernedMetaLearningRecommendation`), but provides no structured gateway to evaluate their validity, relevance, or authority claims.
* **Solution (Component 1159):** The `StrategicAdvisoryMediationRegistry` validates recommendations against strict admission rules: must reference valid MS-1.5.18 records, must have valid provenance hashes, must have explicit `advisory: true` markers, must be bound to authenticated tenants/missions, and must pass security sanitization.

### GAP B: Policy Evolution Without Policy Authority
* **Gap:** Adapting policies based on historical operational experience is necessary for long-term optimization, but allowing an autonomous agent to rewrite its own rules creates existential safety risks.
* **Solution (Components 1160 & 1165):** The `PolicyMutationFirewall` structurally decouples *proposal preparation* from *policy authorization*. MS-1.5.19 can only emit immutable, non-authoritative deliberation dossiers. Only `MasterHumanAuthority` can approve, and only the `PolicyDecisionPoint (PDP)` can activate policies.

### GAP C: Bounded Counterfactual Policy Simulation
* **Gap:** Humans cannot easily predict the systemic repercussions of adjusting complex multi-agent parameters (e.g., lease durations, consensus quorums, conflict thresholds).
* **Solution (Component 1162):** The `CounterfactualPolicySimulationEngine` runs hypothetical policy changes against frozen historical snapshots (from MS-1.5.18 and MS-1.5.17) in a read-only sandboxed graph. It measures projected changes in conflict rates, convergence speed, and compliance drift—without executing any external code or mutating state.

# SECTION 7 — POLICY IMPACT ANALYSIS & BLAST RADIUS ENGINE

Component 1161 (`StrategicPolicyImpactAnalysisEngine`) evaluates the operational and structural consequences of proposed policy modifications across 10 canonical dimensions:

### 10 Canonical Impact Dimensions:
1. `FEDERATION_BLAST_RADIUS`: Number and topology of agent federations affected by the change.
2. `MISSION_SCOPE_SPREAD`: Number of active and queued missions whose constraints would shift.
3. `LEASE_SAFETY_MARGIN`: Impact on autonomy lease boundaries, durations, and privilege envelopes.
4. `RESOURCE_BUDGET_VOLATILITY`: Projected changes in CPU, memory, and token consumption rates.
5. `CONVERGENCE_STABILITY`: Historical sensitivity of cross-federation convergence to this policy field.
6. `CONFLICT_RATE_PROJECTION`: Projected frequency of inter-federation or inter-agent conflict.
7. `REVERSIBILITY_RATING`: Qualitative score (1.0 = trivial rollback, 0.0 = completely irreversible).
8. `DEPENDENT_POLICY_COUPLING`: Upstream and downstream policies coupled via dependency graphs.
9. `DRIFT_ACCELERATION_RISK`: Likelihood of accelerating strategic, compliance, or tenant drift.
10. `SECURITY_PERIMETER_IMPACT`: Any modification touching authentication, authorization, or isolation.

### Scoring & Reversibility Formulation:
* **Composite Impact Score:** $S_{impact} = \max(D_i) \times 0.6 + \text{mean}(D_i) \times 0.4$, bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$.
* If $S_{impact} > 0.70$ or Reversibility $< 0.40$, the proposal is classified as `CRITICAL_RISK` and requires elevated single-human cryptographic affirmation before PDP handoff. Classification does not itself grant approval or execution authority.
